Meet the Author

Crafting RAG Systems for the Real World

This playbook captures years of experimentation launching RAG products in finance, healthcare, and enterprise productivity. It bridges engineering rigor with product design empathy to help you deliver trusted intelligence.

Why this book now?

RAG has moved from prototype to production, yet builders still struggle with repeatable patterns for quality, governance, and iteration. This guide distills the playbooks developed across dozens of launches.

Who is it for?

Architects, ML engineers, product strategists, and technical leaders who need a connective blueprint that unifies infrastructure choices, evaluation loops, and user-centric experience design.

How is it structured?

Organized into foundational components, advanced modalities, and operational excellence, every chapter includes worksheets, code snippets, and checklists that map directly to your roadmap.
