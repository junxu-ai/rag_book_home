Blueprint

The Essential Components of a Modern RAG Pipeline

Trace the lifecycle of your knowledge assets—from first-mile ingestion to observability in production. Each component is grounded in operational patterns and guardrails explored in the book.

Data Processing

Acquire, normalize, and enrich structured and unstructured sources with attention to freshness, access controls, and data minimization.

Vector & Embedding

Choose modality-aware embeddings, apply dimensionality reduction where needed, and track embedding drift with golden datasets.

Prompt Engineering

Structure prompts with deterministic scaffolds, instructions, and explicit guardrails—instrumented with prompt-versioning metadata.

Retrieval

Combine vector search, sparse signals, and business-specific heuristics to maximize recall without sacrificing explainability.

Augmentation

Inject retrieved evidence into prompts through templating, graph context assembly, or fusion-in-decoder pipelines.

Generation

Tune model selection, decoding parameters, and content filters to deliver truthful, brand-safe, and tone-aligned responses.

Evaluation

Blend human review, automatic metrics, adversarial probes, and regression harnesses to validate factual grounding and UX quality.

Serving & Monitoring

Instrument latency SLOs, hallucination detectors, and safety policies across the full serving stack to catch regressions early.

Orchestration & Pipeline

Coordinate schedulers, feature stores, workflow engines, and human-in-the-loop review cycles to keep the pipeline continuously improving.
